# Welcome to VELQ

You’re trying to build a future while keeping up with the degree in front of you. Applications land between lectures. Tests appear in your inbox. Another rejection can make you question a process you were never properly taught.

If that feels familiar, you are not the only one.

## The pressure is real. It is not a verdict on your potential.

The Institute of Student Employers’ 2026 survey reports 172 applications per graduate vacancy in London and 158 in financial and professional services. These are separate averages, not a London-finance acceptance rate or your personal odds. Hiring in the sample grew 4%: opportunities remain, even in a competitive market.

Source: ISE Student Recruitment Report 2026, published 14 October 2026; 155 surveyed employers, 2025/26 recruitment cycle.
https://ise.org.uk/knowledge/insights/615/graduate_jobs_stabilise_but_ai_use_disrupts_recruitment

AI can make it easier to produce applications quickly. It can also make applications sound interchangeable. Your task is to communicate real evidence and sound judgement, follow each employer’s rules and learn from the process.

The cost of university can add another layer of pressure. Your financial circumstances are personal; VELQ will not treat a loan balance or a rejection as a measure of your worth. You do not have to solve your whole career in one evening.

## A place to start, whatever your stage

Spring weeks: understand the work, explore teams and check the programme’s eligibility.

Internships: connect your interests to evidence and prepare for the actual recruitment stages.

Placements: understand the responsibilities and confirm how the year fits your degree.

Graduate roles: bring your evidence together, build a focused shortlist and prepare around the nearest deadline.

If you are switching direction, lack formal experience or are still uncertain, start with the work itself. Coursework, part-time responsibilities, societies, caring, volunteering and independent projects can contain useful evidence. Do not invent experience you do not have.

## Here is the approach

Understand the industry. Choose where to focus. Build a credible application. Practise the next stage. Review what you learn.

You do not need to do everything today. You need to know what matters next—and complete it well.

Use the roadmap below to choose one useful task. Leave the session with a tangible output and a next action. You can return as your priorities change.

The resources provide a preparation process, not a promise of interviews, offers or automatic career matching. Use current official employer information and your university’s careers support alongside VELQ.
