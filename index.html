Finance recruitment can involve overlapping deadlines, role-specific applications, assessments and interviews. VELQ is being built to help aspiring finance professionals approach that process with a repeatable system.

01 / DIRECTION

Choose where to focus

Understand differences between finance role families and make more deliberate decisions about which opportunities fit your interests and evidence.

02 / APPLICATIONS

Build a repeatable workflow

Use structured steps to research roles, tailor application materials and keep track of deadlines and next actions.

03 / INTERVIEWS

Prepare to think, not recite

Develop authentic experience stories, practise explaining your reasoning aloud and improve through targeted follow-up questions.

04 / EXECUTION

Stay organised

Bring applications, assessments, events and follow-ups into a clearer system so important next steps are easier to see.

For students and aspiring professionals navigating UK finance internships, placement years and graduate roles.

Practical preparation.
Honest expectations.

VELQ focuses on frameworks, planning and practice. It does not promise interviews, offers or guaranteed screening outcomes. Application and interview decisions remain with employers.

VELQ is an independent educational project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any employer, university or recruitment platform. Resources are being prepared for launch; this page does not imply that every product is currently available.
