04 / PREPARE FOR WHAT’S NEXT

A better session starts with the actual format and ends with one thing to improve. Explore a sample preparation route.

SAMPLE SESSION / 35 MINUTES

Find the mistake.
Then fix the pattern.

Accuracy comes before speed. Use the employer’s named provider and official familiarisation material where available.

  1. Refresh percentages, ratios, units and chart reading.
  2. Attempt a short set under the stated test rules.
  3. Separate calculation, interpretation and timing errors.
  4. Rework missed questions before doing another set.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Clear expectations.
From the start.

Is VELQ for me if I’m still exploring?

Yes. Begin with the role map and the work different teams do. You can choose a first area to explore without committing to a lifelong career decision.

What if I’m already applying?

Start with your biggest bottleneck: choosing targets, tailoring, assessments or interviews. Use the relevant guide, produce one useful output and record your next action.

Will this help me prepare for assessment centres?

The membership contains an assessment-centre field guide and interview playbook. They cover preparation, case and group exercises, presentations and reviewing your performance. Always confirm the actual format with the employer.

Does it include coaching or application reviews?

This is a self-directed resource membership. Individual CV reviews, coaching calls and recruiter introductions are not included.

Is there a live opportunities tracker?

The downloadable application tracker organises applications you add yourself. A live vacancies directory and smaller-firm outreach list are separate projects in development and are not included in the current launch offer.

Can the role explorer tell me my ideal career?

No. It ranks areas to research using your stated interests. It is an exploratory tool, not a validated aptitude test, hiring prediction or career decision. Your answers are processed in your browser and are not sent to VELQ.

Can VELQ guarantee an interview or offer?

No. VELQ helps structure your preparation. Employers make hiring decisions. Use official employer information and your university careers service alongside the resources.
