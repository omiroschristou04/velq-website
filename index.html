05 / Good to know

A few things
before you
begin.

A little clarity goes a long way.

Who is VELQ for?

Students and aspiring professionals applying for UK finance internships, placement years and graduate roles, whether you're exploring options or already deep in the application cycle.

Does VELQ guarantee interviews or offers?

No. VELQ provides educational resources and preparation frameworks. Employers make their own selection decisions, and no tool or method can guarantee a particular outcome.

Will this tell me to apply to as many roles as possible?

No. The aim is to help you make informed decisions about fit, priorities and preparation capacity—not to treat application volume as a universal benchmark.

Are all resources available right now?

This site introduces the VELQ approach. The product library and launch offers will be clearly described on the storefront as they become available.
