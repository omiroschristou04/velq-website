This review site

This is a private VELQ design and product prototype. It does not accept payments, create VELQ member accounts or offer a production profile service.

Role explorer and session planner

The role explorer calculates results in your browser page. The session planner also runs locally. Neither tool sends your answers to VELQ, writes them to cookies, local storage or a database, or attaches them to an account. Refreshing or closing the page clears this page’s in-memory result. Your browser may restore form entries using its own features.

If you choose to download your exploration notes, your browser saves a text file to your device. VELQ does not receive a copy.

Page delivery

The site host receives normal requests needed to serve the pages and may process service logs and access information. This private preview may require ChatGPT sign-in through the hosting provider. Some pages request fonts from Google Fonts; those font requests share normal connection information with that service. No advertising pixels, email capture or VELQ analytics scripts have been added to this prototype.

Whop

Paid membership resources are managed separately on Whop. This preview is not a Whop checkout and does not change any existing Whop account, billing or membership settings.

Before member profiles are launched

The owner must approve the data fields, purpose and retention period; establish the appropriate legal basis and privacy notice; implement authentication, access controls, deletion and export; and test the service. This prototype does not claim that member-profile collection is ready.

This page describes the current prototype. It is not the final business privacy policy, refund policy or membership contract. Those require the approved business identity and support contact before public launch.
