How are the results calculated?

Each of the ten interests is assigned a manually chosen weight of 1–5 for each role. Your 1–5 answer is shifted to 0–4 and multiplied by that weight. The total is divided by four times the sum of the role’s weights, then multiplied by 100 and rounded.

Equal scores are ties. Ties appear in the order used in the industry map and have no ranking significance. If every answer is the same, all roles tie. Small score differences should not drive a career decision. Many roles vary substantially by employer and team.
