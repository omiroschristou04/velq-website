How the role explorer works.

This is an editorial interest-reflection exercise, not a validated test. Each theme is weighted from 1 (less central in this simplified model) to 5 (more central). The work varies by employer; these weights require user testing and specialist review before any stronger matching claim.

Index = 100 × Σ(weight × (answer − 1)) ÷ (4 × Σweights). Displayed indices are rounded. Ties follow the industry map order, with no significance. Uniform answers give equal results. The index does not measure aptitude, eligibility or probability of an offer.
